1. I changed the LIVE-mode behaviour so that removing the hand produces white light after approximately 0.3 seconds. LOCK mode continues to preserve the selected colour.

2. I reduced the range from 5–40 cm to 5–30 cm because keeping the hand aligned at longer distances was difficult. I also updated the colour mapping to cover the full gradient within the shorter range.

3. Originally, after entering LOCK mode, the user had to move their hand to a different distance before another hover could unlock the colour. If you happen to put your hand back in its original position, the light will not unlock.
   I added an alternative: removing the hand for approximately 0.6 seconds now rearms the gesture. The user can then return to the same position and hover for two seconds to unlock.
